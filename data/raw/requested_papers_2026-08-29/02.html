Beyond Backbones: Degradation-Aware Prototype Fusion for Robust Deepfake Detection

Aashish Negi, Hardik Sharma, Prateek Shaily, Jayant Kumar, Sachin Chaudhary, Akshay Dudhane, Praful Hambarde, Amit Shukla; Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR) Workshops, 2026, pp. 2227-2235

Abstract

Deepfake detectors that perform well on clean images often fail under real-world degradations such as compression, blur, and noise, exposing critical robustness gaps. We address this challenge with a degradation-aware dual-backbone framework that leverages complementary strengths of semantic (CLIP) and structural (DINOv3) representations. The CLIP branch is adapted with LoRA and enhanced via Generator-Aware Prototype Learning (GAPL), while the DINOv3 branch incorporates anomaly-aware local pooling for fine-grained artifact detection. To enforce robustness, we introduce dual-view consistency training across clean and degraded samples, extending corruption diversity from 12 to 26 types. A lightweight fusion module conditioned on image-level degradation descriptors integrates both streams, with rank-based calibration for stable prediction. Our method trains fewer than 3M parameters on frozen backbones and attains 84.3 AUC on the NTIRE 2026 face forgery benchmark, demonstrating strong performance under realistic degradations.
