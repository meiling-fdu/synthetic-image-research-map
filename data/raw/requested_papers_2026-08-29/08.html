ForenDeX: Unlocking Forensic Insights for Explainable AI-Generated Image Detection

Chuangchuang Tan, Jinglu Wang, Xiang Ming, Renshuai Tao, Yunchao Wei, Yao Zhao, Yan Lu; Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR) Findings, 2026, pp. 6592-6601

Abstract

Recent advances in generative models have led to AI-generated images that are visually indistinguishable from authentic ones. While numerous methods have been proposed to detect AI-generated images, these techniques often struggle to provide explanations that align with human cognitive forensic analysis. A significant limitation of existing methods is the reliance on generic features that do not fully capture the authenticity cues inherent in image content. We introduce ForenDeX, a novel framework that addresses this gap by extracting forensic information directly from image content, which is then integrated into a large language model (LLM) as an additional input. Unlike traditional detection methods, which primarily rely on artifact-based features, ForenDeX identifies critical authenticity-related cues such as unnatural textures, lighting inconsistencies, and spatial anomalies, which are often overlooked by current LLM-based approaches. By integrating these contentbased forensic insights, ForenDeX improves both the detection accuracy and the explainability of the model's predictions. Additionally, we introduce ForgReason, a dataset containing AI-generated images annotated with descriptions of authenticity-related features. This dataset, curated through collaboration between human annotators and an LLM-based agent, enables ForenDeX to finetune its ability to detect and explain forgeries, significantly improving the model's performance. Our experiments demonstrate that even limited manual annotations can substantially enhance the quality of the explanations generated by the model. We evaluate ForenDeX on two major benchmarks and show that it outperforms existing detection methods in both accuracy and explainability. Comprehensive evaluations confirm that ForenDeX provides human-understandable explanations by integrating forensic information directly into the model's analysis process.
