## 2. Strict research scope

The Synthetic Image Research Map focuses on passive forensic analysis of AI-generated images.

Include:

- Synthetic Image Detection (SID): real vs. AI-generated images, including fully synthetic faces.
- Synthetic Image Attribution (SIA): generator identification, generator-family attribution, architecture attribution, open-set attribution, and source verification.
- Generative Editing Localization: localization of regions created or modified using generative models.
- Directly relevant datasets, benchmarks, surveys, theoretical analyses, and forensic representation studies.
- Mixed-task forensic papers when synthetic-image detection or generative-editing localization is a substantive, experimentally supported component.
- Passive extraction of generator fingerprints, spectral traces, reconstruction artifacts, and latent representations.

Exclude:

- Watermark embedding, active provenance, or active fingerprint injection into generated outputs.
- Purely conventional splicing/copy-move image forensics without a substantive generative-image component.
- Video-only, audio-only, or text-only deepfake detection.
- Face-swapping-only research without relevant synthetic-image generation or generative editing experiments.
- Generic AI content identification without technical image-forensic evidence.

Do not broaden the project's existing taxonomy or override historical exclusions merely to reach a desired count.
