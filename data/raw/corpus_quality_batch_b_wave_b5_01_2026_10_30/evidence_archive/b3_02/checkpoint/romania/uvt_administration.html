Chancellor's office

The Rector's Office (CR) is the structure of the West University of Timișoara that ensures the institutional interface between the Rector of UVT and the external academic, public and private environment, as well as the operational one in order to apply internal decisions at the level of the institution.

The main purpose is to facilitate internal communication in order to transmit and operationalize the decisions of the UVT Rector, the decisions of the Board of Directors, the Steering Committee, the UVT Senate and to ensure their support and transparency.

The Chancellor's Office (CR) carries out the following activities:

  • Ensures the operational link between the Rector of UVT and Vice-Chancellors/Heads of Departments/Heads of Support Services/students/members of the academic community, as needed.
  • Informs the Rector of UVT about the institutional addresses received by registry/E-mail/fax and keeps track of them, ensuring the traceability of the documents.
  • manages The single register of decisions issued by the UVT Rector and forwards them to the persons/departments/faculties/services directly concerned.
  • Manages the support activity of the Board of Directors, the Steering Committee and the Senate of UVT, in accordance with the Regulation on the organization and operation of the Board of Directors of UVT and the Regulation on the organization and operation of the Senate of UVT.
  • It makes public the decisions of the Board of Directors, the Steering Committee and the UVT Senate by sending them to the IT department for display on the UVT website.
  • It ensures the transmission of the convener and the decisions of the Board of Directors and the UVT Senate to the members of the Board of Directors, the Steering Committee, the members of the Senate, the secretariats of the faculties and the administrative departments directly involved in their operationalization.
  • Sends the convener for the meetings of the Steering Committee, the Board of Directors and the UVT Senate.
  • Forwards to the UVT Senate and specialized commissions all regulations and methodologies aimed at the organization and operation of the university, in order to ensure institutional capacity, educational efficiency and quality management.
  • Ensures the transmission of draft decisions of the Board of Administration, for approval, to the UVT Senate.
  • It provides logistical, material and human support in the organization of events, at the level of the UVT Rectorate, and manages the activity of awarding honorary titles and distinctions (Doctor Honoris Causa) within UVT.

Directorate of Heritage Records, Acquisitions and Investment Monitoring

Department website
Public procurement
Legislation

The Department of Heritage Records, Acquisitions and Investment Monitoring has the mission of supporting the university's management in achieving the proposed objectives regarding the provision of suitable logistical conditions for carrying out teaching, research and administrative activities by designing and permanently adjusting a framework for transposing the decisions of the university's management regarding the efficient use of public funds.

The general objective of the department is to find the balance point, the point where the needs of current operation, development and modernization of activities specific to the administrative, didactic and research fields are satisfied at a higher level possible depending on the limitations given by the annual budget allocations/provisions.

The main specific objectives of the direction are:

    • Carrying out procurement activities regarding investment objectives, investment works, repair/service works and carrying out endowments according to the needs imposed by ensuring the good functioning of all faculties and functional departments within the university;
    • Scheduling the completion of all repairs, modernizations, investments in accordance with the completed projects and based on the budget provisions approved by the university management and/or the competent courts;
    • Ensuring the representation of the component structures in relations with the other departments and the university management;
    • Coordination of the preparation of documents specific to each structure, intended for intra- and interdepartmental correspondence;
    • Elaboration and/or approval of regulatory procedures and norms for the activities within the scope of the department;
    • Ensuring participation in the development of regulatory procedures and norms regarding the general functioning of the university;

General objective: Ensuring the functionality of existing software systems and building a coherent architecture aimed at developing a high-performance, modular and complex digital ecosystem at the UVT level.

Specific objectives:

  • The development and implementation of the digital transformation strategy of UVT, ensuring the coherence of the electronic resources, platforms, applications and information technologies of the Western University of Timișoara, which support the performance and excellence in the academic and research activity of the university;
  • Consistently optimizing and updating workflows to consistently ensure up-to-date and functional software tools for the academic community;
  • Coordinating the retrieval of data and the completion of periodic reports managed at a central level, such as the data reported in ANS, PCUe, data requested by MEC, INS as well as other authorities, with the support of the relevant departments in UVT;
  • Data management and tasks falling within the scope of personal data (GDPR), constant analysis and updating of work procedures to ensure compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679;
  • Management of IT and electronic systems of UVT (UMS, SEAD, admission platform, online accommodation platform, online timetables, SIMD, other relevant projects at UVT level);
  • The development and management of automation systems specific to university activities in close collaboration with relevant departments;
  • Management of systems and platforms accessible through a web browser interface or dedicated application at the UVT level;
  • Support for the functionality of ICT technologies implemented in UVT, used by students, teaching staff, administrative staff and UVT management;
  • Periodic auditing of the level of performance of digital resources and platforms and, depending on the problems identified, proposing measures to correlate these resources with the needs of the teaching staff and students.

The DRI objectives are in line with UVT's international cooperation policy:

  • the development and implementation of the UVT internationalization strategy as a multi- and inter-disciplinary higher education institution;

  • coordination of admission for international students from EU countries and third EU countries;
  • logistical and informational support regarding the development and implementation of cooperation projects with international partners under the umbrella of the Erasmus+ program;
  • development and implementation of a strategy for strengthening UVT's relationship with the Diaspora;
  • contributes to the actions and objectives of UVT within the UNITA alliance of European universities;
  • promoting UVT internationally as a center of excellence in education and research.

Directorate of Educational Management (DME)

Legislation

The general objective of the Educational Management Directorate is to initiate, carry out, support or develop those activities that lead to improving the quality of the educational process, supporting the educational path of students and trainees within the West University of Timișoara, as well as to developing the academic career of teaching staff and their continuous training and professional development.

The organizational structure of the Educational Management Directorate is as follows:

  • Quality Management Service and Support for the Educational Process (SMCSPE) fulfills the role of "technical-administrative and support structure to facilitate the management of internal and external quality assurance and assessment activities", supporting the activity of the Quality Assessment and Assurance Commission (CEAC) of UVT, according to article 112, paragraph (2) from the Higher Education Law no. 199/2023, with subsequent amendments and additions.
    • Department for Distance and Part-Time Education – fulfills the role of a specialized structure that ensures the initiation, development and management of study programs in distance and part-time education, according to the standards in force.
    • Center for Academic Development (CDA) (compartment)
  • Student Counseling and Support Service (SCSS) fulfills the role of career counseling and guidance center as defined in the Methodology regarding the organization and operation of career counseling and guidance centers in higher education institutions approved by the Order of the Minister of Education no. 4042 of March 22, 2024.
    • Department of Inclusion and Support for Students with Special Needs (CISSNS) – fulfills the role of a specialized academic structure for the provision of inclusion services, in the academic environment, for people with disabilities/special educational needs, defined according to the Order of the Minister of Education No. 4481 of May 22, 2024 for the approval of the Norms regarding the accessibility of higher education for people with disabilities.
  • Service for Relations with the Pre-University Environment (SRMP) deals with the development and consolidation of institutional relations between UVT and the pre-university environment. Its main role is to coordinate promotion, educational orientation and counseling activities aimed at students, parents and teachers in pre-university education, in order to facilitate their access to information and opportunities for academic and professional development. SRMP organizes programs and events that promote the educational offer of UVT, initiates strategic partnerships with pre-university education units, supports the preparation of students for the transition to higher education and facilitates their involvement in academic and extracurricular activities of the university. It also ensures constant dialogue with representatives of the pre-university environment, contributing to the creation of sustainable and effective connections between UVT and local and regional educational communities.

General Secretariat Directorate

Study Documents Department → Specific objectives

Specific objectives:

Listing, completing and verifying diplomas and study certificates of graduates of the West University of Timisoara, as well as of graduates of other higher education institutions who took and passed the graduation exam at the West University of Timisoara;

Issuing study documents to holders or their proxies, issuing duplicates, as well as preparing reports on the status of study documents at the West University of Timișoara.

Ensuring the recording, selection, preservation and archiving of educational documents (bachelor's, master's, doctoral degrees, postgraduate specialization and improvement diplomas, DPPD certificates, advanced study diplomas, college graduation diplomas, professional conversion diplomas, graduation diplomas of the psychopedagogical and methodological specialization course, SISEC postgraduate studies, certificates attesting to professional skills, etc.);

The Center for Information and Documentation Resources (CRID) and Romanians everywhere → Specific objectives

Ensuring information and counseling for foreign citizens and Romanians everywhere regarding the admission procedures, recognition and equivalence of educational documents, as well as the regulations applicable to higher education in Romania;

Verification, endorsement and reporting of academic records and authenticity documents, in accordance with the regulations in force;

Managing and submitting admission files of foreign citizens and Romanians from all over the world, in accordance with institutional procedures and applicable legal provisions.

Faculty secretariats → Specific objectives

Specific objectives:

Verification of documents resulting from the schooling process of students and graduates from the first cycle of undergraduate studies and the second cycle of master's studies;

Verification of the correctness and completeness of university documents related to schooling (enrollment register, exam catalogs, transcripts, certificates, etc. as well as statistics requested by the Ministry of Education, CNFIS, the National Institute of Statistics and other authorized institutions);

Managing admission activities and organizing final exams for the two cycles of university studies;

Ensuring the recording, preservation, selection, archiving and disposal of documents at the faculty level, including the organization of archive repositories.

Student InfoCenter Service → Specific objectives

Ensuring communication with UVT students within administrative processes;

Promptly receiving and resolving requests regarding student tuition, sent directly (face to face), by e-mail or through other communication channels, as well as ensuring an appropriate climate for the smooth conduct of the activity;

Management of student files;

Issuance of university documents requested by students.

The General Secretariat Directorate has the following general objectives:

Ensuring the management of information regarding students and their school situation, as well as communication and direct relationship with students, in accordance with the provisions of the Higher Education Law no. 199/2023 and other national regulations, respectively the internal regulations and methodologies of the West University of Timișoara;

Management, completion and issuance of study documents, in accordance with the legislation regarding the regime of study documents in the higher education system;

Ensuring the recording, inventory, selection, preservation, storage and use of internal documents of the West University of Timisoara, in accordance with the legislation and regulations in force.
