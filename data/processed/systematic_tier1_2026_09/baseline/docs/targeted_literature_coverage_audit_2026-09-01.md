# Targeted literature coverage audit — 2026-09-01

## Outcome

The audit covered **22 requested candidate papers** and one separate CatAID venue correction. Before curation, **14 candidates already existed** in the authoritative public paper database and **8 were missing**. The eight missing papers were added through the curated-paper, author–institution mapping, institution-review, and public-export pipeline. No requested candidate was ambiguous or excluded, and no duplicate paper was created.

The eight additions are: **Detecting Generated Images by Fitting Natural Image Distributions**; **Denoising Trajectory Biases for Zero-Shot AI-Generated Image Detection**; **Breaking Latent Prior Bias in Detectors for Generalizable AIGC Image Detection**; **Epistemic Uncertainty for Generated Image Detection**; **Detecting Synthetic Image by Cross-Modal Commonality Interaction**; **Frequency-Aware Correlation Discovering and Spatial Forgery Clue Distilling for Synthetic Image Detection**; **Towards Good Generalizations for Diffusion Generated Image Detection Using Multiple Reconstruction Contrastive Learning**; and **A Difference-in-Difference Approach to Detecting AI-Generated Images**.

Public paper count: **574 → 582**. Published-only count (`conference`, `journal`, or `book`): **512 → 520**.

All 22 candidates are method papers within synthetic/AI-generated image detection or source attribution. The task is `detection` except SemGIR, DE-FAKE, and Forensic Self-Descriptions (`detection_and_source_attribution`) and Where Did I Come From? (`source_attribution`). No model-parsing, hyperparameter-provenance, or broader provenance-extension paper was added.

## Matching procedure

Each requested title was checked against the pre-edit authoritative paper export and curated tables using normalized exact title, DOI, arXiv identifier, known method name, and a normalized fuzzy-title comparison. DOI and arXiv matches were treated as stronger identifiers than title similarity. The eight additions had no matching title, identifier, method-name record, or credible fuzzy match. Every pre-existing candidate had an exact normalized-title match; identifiers were then checked independently. The post-export database contains exactly one identity for every requested candidate.
