## Defect and correction

The frontend renderer initially set the native confirmation button disabled, but its change handler enabled it unconditionally. Confirmation also relied on incomplete candidate state checks, and the modal action lacked clear disabled styling. One eligibility predicate now requires selectable=true and zero conflicts; synchronization additionally binds the selection to the current result set and institution. Selection, render, re-query, close, and application clear or revalidate that state. Native disabled and aria-disabled agree. CSS targets only #geocode-confirm:disabled.

The backend recorded region conflicts but excluded them from the selectable decision; city differences were ranking penalties, and coordinate proximity tolerated tens of kilometres. Every material conflict now blocks selection. Exact locality comparisons have limited explicit spelling/hierarchy exceptions: Sicily/Sicilia, Chinese city suffixes, geographically bounded Hong Kong municipal containment, and central London administrative containment. Kowloon and New Territories remain distinct. Confirmed coordinates take precedence over imported mapping coordinates; candidates more than 2 km away require manual evidence. A relation boundary with diagonal greater than 5 km is conservatively blocked pending campus-specific verification. This size threshold is a review heuristic, not proof that every broad relation is multi-campus. Raw addresses, bounds, and OSM identity remain attached to candidates.

HKBU relation 15525646 contains Shaw, Shek Mun, Tsuen Wan, Kai Tak and other campus objects. Its Sha Tin/New Territories address cannot identify the Kowloon Tong campus. The TMLR Group affiliation was also being misparsed as a locality. Official CS contact evidence locates the relevant unit on Shaw Campus. The confirmed point is an area centroid inside Shaw campus way 236995988 (22.337924, 114.1826302), not the aggregate relation point. Melbourne similarly uses the verified Parkville main-campus polygon 1156270658. Centroids were computed with the polygon shoelace formula and checked inside the chosen outer polygon. They represent campuses, not exact offices.
